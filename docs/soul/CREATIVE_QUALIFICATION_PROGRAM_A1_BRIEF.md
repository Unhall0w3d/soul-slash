# Creative Qualification Program A1 Brief

## Objective

Expose one read-only Music Studio qualification ledger for the reviewed
43-, 57-, 71-, 144-, and 248-second liminal cohort. Use the same evidence-led
review period to complete Music Studio finishing evaluation and the existing
Visual Motion qualification decision without merging their authority.

## Boundary

- Read existing immutable project, candidate, artifact, timing, and human-review
  records only.
- Do not generate, revise, trim, export, delete, bind, release, or publish.
- Do not infer aesthetic approval from technical evidence, model output, or an
  existing `keep` disposition.
- Keep 301–599-second Music Studio targets rejected; the separately qualified
  600-second lane remains unchanged.
- Keep Visual Motion qualification in its existing independent ledger.
- No worker, queue, watcher, service, scheduler, or background polling is added.

## Music evidence

For each target duration the ledger reports project and candidate counts,
technical FLAC duration/sample/channel evidence, review coverage, current
candidate state, revision lineage, generation time, and whether any retained
candidate has a `keep` review. Free-form review notes remain evidence for the
Operator; the service does not parse them into approval.

## Acceptance

- Missing, unreviewed, revision-required, and archive-attention states are
  deterministic and visible.
- Exact 43-, 57-, 71-, 144-, and 248-second evidence is distinguishable.
- A technically valid artifact requires a non-empty FLAC within two seconds of
  target duration at 48 kHz stereo.
- The Dashboard refresh is manual and read-only.
- Existing Music, Visual, Core, generation, revision, and review gates remain
  unchanged.

## Risk

Risk classification: Class 1 owner-local read. Candidate titles and review
summaries remain on the authenticated local Dashboard. No new durable private
state is created.
